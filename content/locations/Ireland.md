Two photographs survive from Killiney Bay on the south coast of County Dublin, Ireland — a location famous for its views across the bay toward the Wicklow Mountains.

## Killiney Bay

Killiney Bay is a sweeping bay south of Dublin, often compared to the Bay of Naples. Killiney Hill offers panoramic views south over the bay and inland toward the Wicklow Mountains.
